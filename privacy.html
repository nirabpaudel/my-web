Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how I, Nirab Paudel, collect, use, and protect information relating to visitors of my personal website, including nirabpaudel.com, nirab.info.np, nirabpaudel.com.np, and their associated pages and subdomains where this notice is displayed. I believe that visitors should be able to understand what happens to their information without having to navigate through unnecessary legal language. For this reason, I have tried to explain my practices as clearly and transparently as possible.


Browsing this website does not mean that you have agreed to optional analytics, advertising, or other non-essential tracking. Where consent is required, I ask for your permission before enabling those technologies. You may reject optional technologies and continue browsing the website. My Terms and Conditions are available separately.


Last Updated: November 26, 2026


If you have questions about this policy, the information I may hold about you, or how to exercise your privacy rights, please contact me. For the purposes of applicable data protection laws, I am the individual responsible for deciding how personal information submitted directly through this website is used.


Information I Collect


This website primarily exists to share information about me, my interests, and my work. You do not need to create an account, make a purchase, or provide your name to browse its public pages. The information processed depends on how you choose to interact with the website.


Information You Provide: When you contact me, I may receive your name, email address, the content of your message, attachments, and any other information you choose to include. I use this information to read and respond to your inquiry and, when necessary, to continue the conversation.


Newsletter Subscriptions: If you voluntarily subscribe to my newsletter, I may collect your email address and any other details requested on the subscription form. The information you submit is processed through Brevo (previously Sendinblue), the service I use to manage newsletter subscriptions and communications. Subscribing is optional, and you may unsubscribe through the link included in applicable emails. Your decision to subscribe is separate from your choice regarding website cookies.


Information I Do Not Request: I do not operate user accounts or payment processing through this website, and I do not intentionally request financial information, government identification numbers, biometric data, or special categories of personal information. Please avoid including sensitive information in messages unless it is necessary for your inquiry. If you choose to share such information, I will handle it only as reasonably necessary and as permitted by applicable law.


Information Automatically Collected


Like most websites, this website relies on technical services that may process information when a page is requested, even if you reject optional cookies. Depending on the service, this may include your IP address, browser and operating system, device characteristics, referring page, requested URL, time of access, and security or error information. An IP address may provide a general indication of your region, but I do not use this website to request your device's precise GPS location.


Log Files and Website Security


GitHub Pages and Cloudflare are among the services associated with this website's publication, delivery, and security. These providers may process technical request records and security information to deliver pages, maintain network reliability, diagnose errors, detect suspicious activity, and protect the website against misuse. Some of this processing may occur independently of the optional consent settings because it is needed for basic operation and security.


Technical logs and IP addresses can constitute personal information under privacy laws, even when I do not know a visitor's name. I do not treat these records as automatically anonymous. My access to individual records, and the periods for which they are retained, may depend on the services and settings of the relevant hosting or security provider.


Cookies and Privacy Choices


This website uses a privacy consent system that allows you to accept optional technologies, reject them, or select individual categories. Strictly necessary functions, including remembering your privacy choices and protecting the website, can remain active without optional consent. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent basic hosting and security-related network requests.


  • Strictly Necessary: Technologies needed to operate the website, maintain its security, and remember your privacy preferences. The consent manager saves your decision in your browser's local storage under np-privacy-consent-v1, together with your selected categories, the decision date, and an expiration date. The saved preference generally lasts 180 days unless you clear your browser's storage or the policy configuration changes.
  • Analytics: Optional technologies used to understand visits and interactions with the website. Google Tag Manager (GTM) may load Google Analytics or other analytics tags configured within it. Under the site's consent setup, GTM is intended to load only after you permit Analytics. The particular information collected and retention settings depend on the tags that are actually enabled.
  • Functional: Optional third-party features, including scripts that support the Brevo newsletter form. Declining these may limit the behavior of an optional feature without preventing access to the rest of the website.
  • Marketing: Optional advertising or conversion-measurement technologies, if configured in the future or enabled on the website. These must have the appropriate separate permissions before they are allowed to collect information for marketing purposes.

You can change or withdraw your selection at any time by opening Privacy Choices through the website. Withdrawing permission stops the relevant optional technologies from being loaded on subsequent page loads, but it does not reverse processing that already occurred. Some cookies set by third parties may also need to be removed through your browser settings.


Where supported, the consent system recognizes the Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signal and prevents the optional Marketing category from being enabled. GPC does not disable technical processing needed to deliver and secure the website. Browser-level Do Not Track (DNT) settings are not treated as an equivalent substitute for a specific consent decision.


How I Use Your Information


I process information only for purposes connected to operating this website and communicating with people who choose to contact or subscribe to me. These purposes include:


  • Making the website and its public content accessible to visitors.
  • Responding to messages, questions, or other inquiries you choose to send.
  • Managing newsletter subscriptions and sending emails that you requested.
  • Maintaining website security, investigating technical problems, and preventing misuse.
  • Understanding website traffic and improving its content where optional analytics are permitted.
  • Remembering and respecting your privacy choices.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations or responding to valid legal requests where required.

I do not use this website to make decisions about visitors through solely automated processing that produces legal or similarly significant effects.


What Legal Bases Do I Rely on to Process Your Information?


Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or another law requiring a legal basis applies, the basis depends on the specific information and the reason for its processing. I do not treat visiting the website as consent to every possible use of information.


  • Consent: For non-essential analytics and marketing technologies where consent is required, and for newsletter communications to which you voluntarily subscribe. You can withdraw this permission without affecting the lawfulness of processing that took place before withdrawal.
  • Legitimate Interests: For operating, securing, maintaining, and improving the website and responding to ordinary correspondence, where these interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms. This does not replace consent when consent is legally required for cookies or similar technologies.
  • Legal Obligations: Where I am required to retain, disclose, or otherwise process certain information to comply with applicable law.

You are not legally or contractually required to provide contact or newsletter information simply to read the website. If you choose not to provide an email address or a message, I may not be able to respond to you or send the newsletter you requested.


When and With Whom Is Information Shared?


I use third-party services to host website content, protect the website, display certain resources, manage subscriptions, and, when permitted, understand visitor activity. These services may process information in accordance with their respective roles, terms, and privacy policies. Their involvement does not mean that I intentionally sell visitors' contact details or browsing information.


  • Brevo (Previously Sendinblue): For newsletter registration, subscription management, and related emails. Information you provide through the subscription form may be transferred to Brevo. You can read Brevo's Privacy Policy.

  • GitHub Pages: For publishing and delivering the static website. GitHub may process technical information associated with requests to pages or files. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

  • Cloudflare: For website delivery, network functionality, protection against abuse, and potentially technical or aggregated traffic reporting. This processing may take place independently of Google Analytics. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Fonts: Certain pages request font resources from Google's servers. This can involve the transmission of technical request information, including your IP address, to Google when those resources load. This request is separate from optional Google Analytics tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics: If you give Analytics permission, GTM may load configured analytics services to help me understand website traffic. Depending on the configuration, these services may process device identifiers, page interactions, approximate location, and other usage information. See Google's Privacy Policy. Analytics and advertising permissions are not the same choice.

  • Website Resources: Some scripts, styles, images, or other resources may be delivered from nirab.info.np or other domains used by the website. When your browser requests a third-party resource, the provider may receive technical connection information. Optional scripts must be reviewed and, when necessary, restricted by consent.


You can find additional technical information about this website in my info.txt file, where available. I may also disclose information if legally required, or if reasonably necessary to protect the website and its visitors within the limits of applicable law.


International Processing and Transfers


I operate this personal website from the United States, and the providers identified above may process information in the United States or other countries. Data protection protections can differ between countries. Where international transfer rules apply, appropriate safeguards must be used for the relevant transfers, such as an applicable adequacy framework or contractual safeguards. If you would like more information about a particular provider or its international transfer arrangements, you may contact me.


Retention of Your Personal Data


I aim to keep personal information only for as long as it is necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. The relevant period or criteria depend on the type of information:


  • Contact Messages: For the period reasonably necessary to respond to your message, maintain relevant correspondence, or address a legitimate legal or security need. You may request deletion, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Newsletter Details: For as long as your subscription remains active, followed by any limited period necessary to record an unsubscribe request or meet applicable legal obligations. Brevo's own retention and technical records are also subject to its practices.
  • Consent Preferences: The browser-stored preference normally expires 180 days after your decision, unless you delete it sooner or a policy change requires a new decision.
  • Hosting and Security Logs: For periods determined by the relevant service settings, its retention practices, and legitimate security requirements.
  • Optional Analytics: According to the retention configuration of the relevant analytics service and only for as long as reasonably necessary to understand website use.

When information is no longer needed, I seek to delete it or have it deleted where feasible. Some backups or records held by service providers may take additional time to expire under their retention schedules.


Third-Party Websites and External Links


My website may link to other websites, social media pages, or services that I do not operate. When you choose to leave my website, the other service's privacy practices apply. I encourage you to review those policies independently. I cannot control how unrelated websites collect or use information that you provide to them.


Children's Information


This is a general personal website and is not specifically directed toward children. I do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13 or otherwise below the applicable age for providing their own consent under local law. If you believe a child has submitted personal information that should not have been collected, please contact me so that I can review the situation and take appropriate action.


What Are Your Privacy Rights?


Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply to the processing in question, you may have rights to access information about yourself, request a copy, correct inaccurate information, ask for deletion, restrict or object to certain processing, and request data portability. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and exceptions.


If you live in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority if you believe your information has been handled unlawfully. Information about the relevant authorities is available through the European Data Protection Board and the UK Information Commissioner's Office. Visitors in Switzerland may contact the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.


Withdrawing Your Consent: You may use Privacy Choices to change optional website tracking preferences, or contact me with another consent-related request. Withdrawing consent does not invalidate processing that was lawful before the withdrawal.


Unsubscribing from Emails: If you subscribe to my newsletter, you may unsubscribe using the link in the email or by contacting me. Rejecting analytics cookies does not automatically unsubscribe you from an email list, and unsubscribing does not automatically change the cookies stored in your browser.


Submitting a Request: You may send requests to support@nirabpaudel.com. I may ask for limited additional information where reasonably necessary to verify your identity or protect another person's information. I will respond within the time required by any law applicable to your request.
